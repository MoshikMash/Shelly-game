📖 Guide for Grown-ups

This app follows systematic synthetic phonics — the method used in schools and proven by reading research to work best for beginners.

1. Sounds before names 🔊

Teach the letter sound first ("mmm"), not the name ("em"). Sounds are what we blend into words. Keep sounds short and clean — say "t", not "tuh" with a big "uh".

2. Follow the letter order 🔤

The letters are grouped in teaching order, starting with s, a, t, p, i, n — these six letters build lots of real words (sat, pin, tap, nap…), so your child can read whole words very quickly.

3. Blend, blend, blend 🧩

In Sound It Out, tap each tile ("c… a… t"), then say the sounds faster and faster until they melt into the word: "cat!" Use "🐢 Sound it out" to model it, and always celebrate with "⭐ I read it!"

4. Little and often ⏰

5–10 minutes a day beats one long weekly session. Stop while it's still fun! Move to the next level only when the current one feels easy.

5. Keep reading aloud 📚

This app teaches decoding — keep reading picture books aloud together every day for vocabulary and the love of stories. Decodable books ("Bob Books", "Songbirds") pair perfectly with each level here.

Tip 🍎

The voice comes from your device's speech engine. If you hear nothing, check the ringer/volume switch and tap any letter once to wake up audio.
